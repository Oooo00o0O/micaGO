Your Mac's iMessage, on your phone and PC.

micaGO lets you read and reply to your iMessages from an Android phone or a Windows PC. It works like a remote control for the Messages app on your own Mac, so you need a Mac that stays switched on.

It's a remote for your Mac Your Mac really sends and receives the messages. micaGO lets you do it from another device.
No Mac, no micaGO You need your own Mac, switched on, with Messages signed in to your Apple ID.
No micaGO servers Messages travel between your devices and your Mac. We don't run a server that sees them.

Is this for me?

Check this before you download.

People often guess micaGO is an app that gives Android phones iMessage on its own. It isn't. It only works together with a Mac you already have.

Good fit: you own a Mac

A Mac at home or at work that is signed in to Messages and can stay on. That Mac is what actually talks to Apple.

Good fit: you also use Android or Windows

You want to keep up with your iMessage chats from an Android phone, tablet, or a Windows computer instead of walking back to the Mac.

Not a fit: you have no Mac

micaGO can't create an iMessage account or connect to Apple by itself. Without a Mac of your own, it won't work.

Not a fit: the Mac is usually off

Messages only come and go while the Mac is on and awake. If it sleeps or shuts down, micaGO waits until it's back.

What you can do

Everyday chatting, from another screen.

Replies are sent by your Mac with your own Apple ID and phone number, exactly as if you had typed them on the Mac. Your friends see nothing different.

Blue-bubble chats

Read and reply to your iMessage conversations, including group chats.

Photos, voice and reactions

Send pictures and voice messages, reply to a message, and add reactions.

Green-bubble texts

Regular text messages (SMS) work too if your iPhone forwards them to your Mac (Text Message Forwarding in the iPhone settings).

Away from home (optional)

On the same Wi-Fi it just works. To use it anywhere, you can set up your own secure link to your Mac. Remote access guide.

How it works

Set up once, in three steps.

1

Set up your Mac

Install the micaGO Companion on the Mac that has Messages signed in. When it asks, allow Full Disk Access so it can see your messages. Mac setup guide.

2

Connect your phone or PC

Install micaGO on Android or Windows, then scan the QR code shown by the Companion. Being on the same Wi-Fi is the easiest way to start. Connection guide.

3

Start chatting

Your conversations appear. Leave the Mac on and it keeps delivering messages both ways.

Privacy

Your messages go between your devices and your Mac. Nowhere else.

There is no micaGO account and no micaGO cloud. We don't operate a server that stores or reads your conversations.

Using micaGO away from home is optional. If you set it up, the link goes to your own Mac and is protected by a secret key only your devices know. Remote access guide.

Push notifications are optional too. If you turn them on, notifications are delivered by Google's push service through a Firebase project you create yourself. Notifications guide.

FAQ

Common questions.

Can I get iMessage on Android without a Mac?

No. micaGO needs your own Mac signed in to Messages. The Mac does the real work.

Does my Mac have to stay on?

Yes. While the Mac is asleep or off, nothing is sent or received. The Companion has a Keep Awake switch to stop the Mac from sleeping.

Will my friends see a different number or name?

No. Messages are sent by your Mac from your own Apple ID and number.

Is micaGO made by Apple?

No. micaGO is an independent open source project and is not affiliated with Apple.

Are my messages stored in a micaGO cloud?

No. There is no micaGO cloud. Messages stay on your Mac and the devices you connect.

Can I use it away from home?

Yes, once you set up your own secure link to your Mac. Read the remote access guide.

Is it stable?

It's beta software. It works day to day, but expect some rough edges.
